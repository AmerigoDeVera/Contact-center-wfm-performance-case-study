# Contact-center-wfm-performance-case-study
An operations case study on faster WFM capacity and KPI-risk analysis.
## Overview

Our contact-center team needed a faster way to compare historical demand,
scheduled staffing, and actual performance across 10 plus products.
The goal was to identify coverage gaps early enough to adjust staffing and
protect service-level KPIs tied to monthly contractual penalties.

This is a high-level description of a team initiative. It does not contain
client data, proprietary workflows, or production dashboards.

## The problem

Answering a weekly staffing question required manually pulling and
reconciling information from multiple systems: Were enough people scheduled
to handle expected call volumes while meeting the required KPIs?

The process consumed significant analyst time. By the time the information
was assembled, leaders had less time to make useful schedule or capacity
adjustments.

## The approach

The team used Alteryx to combine information from sources including a SQL
contact-center data warehouse, WFM data, and call-volume data. The workflow
prepared outputs for a Tableau dashboard that operations and WFM leaders
could use to examine product-level demand, scheduled capacity, actual
performance, and KPI risk.

The operating process was:

1. Bring relevant data together from the available source systems.
2. Compare call demand, planned staffing, and actual results by product
   and time period.
3. Flag potential coverage gaps or KPI risks.
4. Review the findings with WFM and operations leaders.
5. Adjust staffing plans or other operational actions where appropriate.

## Outcomes

- Reduced the data-preparation and reporting cycle from weeks to seconds
  once the workflow was run.
- Removed substantial manual effort previously spent gathering and
  reconciling information across products.
- Gave leaders more timely visibility into staffing gaps and KPI risk.
- Supported proactive adjustments that helped avoid contractual penalties.
